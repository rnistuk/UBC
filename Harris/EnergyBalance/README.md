### Stefan Boltzmann

F = σ * T^4

F is energy output

### First No Sun
 Earth is 32K(-241c) -- dark and cold 
 - Earth emits 0.06 W/m^2
 - 
 Then turn on the Sun
 - Assume no reflection, no greenhouse gases
 - inflow 341W/m^2
 - Earth's surface absorbs 341 W/m^2
 
Then add reflection
- earth reflects about 30% so it only needs to emit 239W/m^2

Then add greenhouse gases
- greenhouse gases absorb all the energy emitted from earth surface
- re-emit 50% up, and 50% down (239W/m^2 up and down)
- energy balance: upward emmission == solar inflow - reflection (239W/m^2)
- in order for energy balance the earths' surface must emit 2x the absorbed, so 
emitted is 478W/m^2
